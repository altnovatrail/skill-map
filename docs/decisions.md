## The data and the element of the map

**R03. The package draws what `api` gives for the map by the contract (R99 spec), and it lays out the graph on the device itself: it needs no fields beyond the contract. The state on the map of a skill with a verdict it derives from the graph and the answer of `GetSkillMap` — there are five of them —, and a skill with a rubric or one that is not assessed has a mark instead of a state. The map is of one module, with the anchor skills before tier 1, and it has two views: the learner's and the adult's. Only a skill that is not locked can be chosen on it. The package holds no learner's data on the device.** The author's answers of 2026-11-07 when T01 was planned (RUN.md, T01, «Решено автором»), all by the plan's recommendations; the rest are the plan's proposals, which the author approved. How it is built is in [architecture.md](architecture.md), sections 3.1 and 3.2.
- **The layout** is the package's, by the open view of the graph and the tiers `skill_tiers` (R62 spec, R99 spec), and it is the same on the same data. Its rule is T08 of RUN.md. The contract has no coordinates.
- **The states on the map** of a skill whose assessability is `verdict` follow its `SkillState` and the graph's prerequisites, the first that fits, in order:
  1. "time to review" — `mastery_band` is not empty, and `review_due`, or `review_date` no later than the time of the device;
  2. "mastered" — `mastery_band` is not empty;
  3. "in progress" — `evidence_count` is greater than zero;
  4. "ready to start" — every prerequisite of the skill among the module's skills whose assessability is `verdict` has a `mastery_band` that is not empty; for a skill without such prerequisites, at once;
  5. "locked" — the rest.

  A mastered prerequisite that it is time to review does not lock the skill: time does not take away "mastered" (concepts 11 spec). A prerequisite with a rubric or not assessed, and an anchor skill of the skill, do not lock it either: the engine keeps the author's order, and whether to hold back a lesson on "not held" is for its rule to decide (concepts 4 spec). The package reads a skill with a verdict that the answer does not name as a skill without evidence. A skill with evidence — "in progress", "mastered" or "time to review" — is open even when its prerequisite is not mastered yet or has already lost "mastered": the learner has already worked on it. "Ready to start" rests on the same as the reason code "a new skill", `REASON_CODE_NEW_SKILL` — «пререквизиты освоены или их нет» ("the prerequisites are mastered, or there are none") ([the text of the contracts](../spec/contracts/README.md), section 15) —, but takes of them only the module's prerequisites with a verdict: the map does not see whether the others are mastered. Whether the map's rule and the engine's rule will coincide is decided by the detailing of `engine` (RUN.md, table 3). The package does not compute mastery: it matches the ready marks of `learner-model` against the graph.
- **Rubric and not assessed.** A skill whose assessability is `rubric` or `none` stands in its tier, with its links, and instead of a state it has a mark — "feedback by criteria" or "not assessed": it has no state in `GetSkillMap` (R99 spec), and it is never "mastered" (concepts 4 spec). An adult sees the marks of the criteria in the progress of `app` (R87 spec), not on the map.
- **The anchor skills** that are the module's prerequisites stand before tier 1 — they have no tier of their own (R49 spec) —, each with its `AnchorSkillState`: "held", "not held" or "unknown", with a reason. A value that the build does not know is "unknown", and a reason that it does not know is not shown (`progress.proto` of `spec`). The title of an anchor skill is English, with `lang="en"` (R50 spec, R91 spec), and where it comes from is R04. What a module's skill teaches, `teaches`, is in its details, with the same state of the anchor skill. The map does not name the skills that teach an anchor skill, `teaching_skills`: they are full IDs of skills of other modules, and the map does not have their names.
- **Choosing.** On the learner's view, a skill that is "time to review", "mastered", "in progress" or "ready to start" can be chosen, and so can a skill with a rubric or not assessed. For a "locked" skill the map names the prerequisites that are not mastered yet, and it cannot be chosen. `app` passes the chosen skill to the engine, `ChooseNext` (R99 spec); how the package gives it out is R04.
- **Two views.** The learner's has the states in words and an icon, not by color alone (R90 spec), and the choice of a skill. The adult's is the same map without the choice, and a skill also has details: the number of pieces of evidence `evidence_count`, the last practice `last_practiced_at`, the mastery band by its title in the module manifest (`bands`) and the review date `review_date` or "time to review". The estimate `estimate`, the uncertainty `uncertainty`, the retention `retention` and the cautious probability `cautious_probability` are shown by neither view. `app` says which view is needed: on the learner's screen the learner's view, in the section for adults the adult's view, and the same for an owner who learns themselves (concepts 13 spec; R26 spec, R84 spec).
- **Without state.** Without an answer of `GetSkillMap` the package draws the graph and the tiers without marks of state and without the choice. This is the map for `site` and the author's tools (architecture 3.3 spec): they have no learner's data.
- **Data on the device.** No `localStorage`, no IndexedDB, no cache: the state of the skills lives in the element's memory while it is on the page. What the device holds is the register of `app` (R04 app; privacy 4 spec), and for the map it does not change. Computing on the device, for offline, is stage 5 (Т-10).

— *Why:*
- the layout depends on the screen, the zoom and the length of the names in the explanation language, and `api` does not know these. The tier already comes ready (R49 spec, R62 spec), and what is left to the layout is the order of the skills in a tier and the lines of the links — as with MathTrail's map of topics ([architecture.md](architecture.md), section 4);
- product 4 spec asks to show what is mastered, what opens and what it rests on: the graph with the marks "mastered" gives this, and "time to review" comes from the review date in the same answer (concepts 11 spec). The rule "ready to start" is taken from the definition of the code "a new skill" in the contract, so that the map and the engine speak of an open skill as closely as possible;
- a skill opens by its prerequisites (concepts 4 spec): a locked skill is seen on the map but cannot be chosen. The map does not lock a skill on prerequisites whose mastery it cannot see — with a rubric, not assessed, anchor skills —: otherwise a skill with a rubric in a chain would lock everything after it for ever;
- the essay module is in the first slice (R41 spec), and almost all its skills are with a rubric. Without them its map would be empty, and the links through such skills would break;
- the level is per module, and each module has its own scale (concepts 11 spec): the tiers of different modules cannot be compared. `GetSkillMap` gives one module (R99 spec), and modules are linked only by anchor skills (concepts 4 spec) — and these are what the map shows;
- the estimate, the uncertainty and the cautious probability are the model's numbers on the scale of the module's bands, and they tell neither the learner nor an adult anything without that scale. The mastery band has its title in the module manifest, and an adult can plan for the review date;
- `canvas` has no data of its own (R36 spec): so here too. Storing the state of the skills on the device would mean a new row in the register of the device's data and a storage period of its own (privacy 4 spec, П-9), and the first slice has no offline (product 10 spec).

— *Rejected:*

- the prototype's "new this week" as a field `mastered_at` in `SkillState`: product 4 spec does not require it, and a field in `v1` cannot be removed later;

So that the map's rule and reachability do not part, the rule goes to `engine` as a row «В реестр spec» (RUN.md, table 3);

- the model's numbers for the adult too: see "Why";

## The layout of the map

**R06. The map is the tiers' bands from top to bottom: before tier 1, the band of anchor skills; the skills in a band wrap, and the page scrolls. The order in a band is by the mean place of the prerequisites. A skill is expanded by a tap, Enter or Space: the details open under it, and lines go only from its prerequisites and to what it opens. Starting a session is the button "Practise" in the details. From the keyboard, Tab through the buttons. Distant tiers do not collapse. The layout is the package's own code, without a library.** The author's answers of 2026-11-07 when planning T08 (RUN.md, T08, «Решено автором»), all by the plan's recommendations; the rest is the plan's proposals, which the author approved. How it is built: [architecture.md](architecture.md), section 3.4.
- **Bands.** A band is a tier, from top to bottom: first the anchor skills, then tier 1, 2 and on, as many as there are (R49 spec). A band has a heading: the word for a tier from the package's table and the number, and after them the tier's name from `tiers`, if it has one — "Tier 2 · Sharing", "Tier 3" (R49 spec); the band of anchor skills has its own. The skills of a band are tiles in a row that wraps. The page of `app` scrolls; the map has no scroll container of its own.
- **The order in a band.** Tier 1 — by a skill's local ID. Each next band — by the mean place of a skill's prerequisites among the module's skills, and a place is the number of a tile in its band, from zero; on a tie, by ID. IDs are compared by character codes. Anchor skills give no place, and in their own band they stand by ID. The layout does not rest on the order of the skills in the graph: it has no meaning ([format text](../spec/format/README.md), section 4). The state and the expansion do not change a tile's place.
- **Anchor skills** in the band are only the prerequisites of the module's skills (R03). If the module has none, there is no band either, with its heading. An anchor skill can be expanded too: the details name its state, its reason and the module's skills that need it.
- **Expansion and details.** A tap, Enter or Space on a tile expands the skill, and a second one closes it; one skill is expanded. Any skill can be expanded, a locked one and an anchor skill too; a skill is chosen for a session only by the button "Practise" (R03, R04). The details stand in the DOM right after the tile, as a separate item of the band's list, and take the whole width of the band — the tiles after the expanded one wrap below them. The details hold the title and the description, the state or the mark, what the skill builds on and what it opens, what it teaches, and for the adult also the numbers of R03. On the learner's view, with `skillMap`, a module's skill that is not locked has the button "Practise", and it gives `skillchoose` (R04), and a locked one has "waits for" and the prerequisites not yet mastered. An anchor skill has no button, nor does any skill on the adult's view, without `view` or with another value of it, or without state (R04). Whatever `ChooseNext` answers — "nothing to give" too, when `api` has limited the pool to tasks without AI (R104 spec) — `app` shows: after the event the map waits for nothing. A tile is a disclosure button: `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` on the details. The expansion lives in the element's memory and does not go outside (R03).
- **New data.** On the assignment of properties the element updates the tiles in place, by skill ID: the tile that has the focus and the expanded details stay. If the expanded skill is gone with the new data, the details close. If the node that has the focus is gone with the new data — a tile, or the button "Practise" that "waits for" replaced —, the focus moves to the tile of the same skill, and without it to the first tile of the same band, and without it to the first tile of the map.
- **Lines.** Only for the expanded skill: from the tile of each of its prerequisites — a module's skill or an anchor skill — to it, and from it to each skill that it is a prerequisite of. While the details are open, the lines to what the skill opens leave from the bottom edge of the details: the tile and the details are one block, and the lines do not run under them. The package draws the lines by the measured places of the tiles after the layout and draws them again when the size changes. The lines lie under the tiles, and each has a dot at both ends. A link across a tier and a line from an anchor skill pass under the tiles of the bands in between, and a line from the details also under the tiles wrapped below them: what is linked to what is told not by the lines but by the dots at the ends and the labels "needed before" and "opens after" on the linked tiles — only on them. So a link is visible, and not by color alone (1.4.1). The contrast of a line with the background is no less than 3:1 (1.4.11). With `prefers-reduced-motion` the lines appear without animation (R90 spec).
- **A sequential module** follows the same rule: one skill per band. It has no look of its own: the label "sequential" is shown by `app` (the 2026-11-02 amendment to R58 spec).
- **Keyboard and focus.** Each tile is an ordinary button in the Tab order: band after band, in the order of the layout. Enter or Space expand the skill, the focus stays on it, and "Practise" is the next Tab stop, because the details stand in the DOM right after the tile. The map has nothing sticky of its own, and `app` answers for the focus not being hidden by something sticky on the page — a header or a bar of `app` (2.4.11).
- **The text alternative** is the DOM tree itself: a band is a heading and a list of tiles, a tile has the accessible name "title, state", and a highlighted one also its label: "title, state, needed before". The label stands inside the tile's button. The links and the details are named in words. The SVG of the lines is `aria-hidden`: everything in it is already said in words. There is no separate list or table. The level of the headings is set by the element's attribute `heading-level` — the 2026-11-07 amendment to R04.
- **Targets** are no less than 44 × 44 CSS px (R90 spec). The state on a tile is a word and an icon (R03). At 320 CSS px and at 200% zoom the bands wrap, and nothing scrolls sideways (1.4.10).
- **No collapsing.** All tiers are always visible. Row Д3 in table 4 of RUN.md — come back to it when a module has more than six tiers.
- **Own code.** A tier comes ready (R62 spec), and what is left to the layout is the order in a band and the lines of one skill — a few dozen lines of code, not a task for a library.

— *Why:*
- `app` scrolls by the page, and it gives a scroll container of its own only with `tabindex="0"`, the role `region` and a name (architecture 3.9 `app`, `main` 375a09b; architecture 6 spec). Bands that wrap need no sideways scrolling at all: they need no exception of 1.4.10 for diagrams, there is no second direction of scrolling and no need for moving without dragging (2.5.7), and at 200% they simply wrap;
- the order of the DOM and the order on the screen match: band after band, tile after tile, the details after the expanded one (1.3.2, 2.4.3). So the tree is the text alternative, and there is no second representation that could drift from the map;
- with wrapping into several rows, all the lines at once would cross and run through other tiles. The lines of one skill show what product 4 spec requires showing — what it builds on and what it opens —, and only when the learner has asked about it. The prototype does so too: the marks "before" and "after" at the chosen topic, without lines (`spec/draft/NovaTrail Widget.html`, `tsteps`);
- `ChooseNext` closes the open attempt, and `app` warns about it ([contracts text](../spec/contracts/README.md), section 15): an accidental tap must not start a session. The expansion and the details, and then the button — like the panel "Practise: …" of the prototype. A tablet has no hover, so the highlight follows the expansion, not the hover;
- the mean place of the prerequisites keeps skills near what they build on, in reading order: a skill whose prerequisites are nearer the start of their bands is nearer the start of its own. It stands under its prerequisite on the screen only while the bands do not wrap — as it was in the columns of the MathTrail topic map ([architecture.md](architecture.md), section 4). The ID gives the same order on the same data;
- in the first slice there are no more than four tiers, and up to 17 skills in a module ([architecture.md](architecture.md), section 3.4): there is nothing to collapse, and Tab through 17 buttons is shorter than learning a way of moving by arrows of the map's own;
- layout libraries lay the graph out in layers themselves and set absolute coordinates ([architecture.md](architecture.md), section 4), while here the layer comes ready, and CSS wraps the tiles.

— *Rejected:*
- all the lines always: with wrapping they tangle;
- columns of tiers from left to right, as in MathTrail, with sideways scrolling in a `region` of its own and buttons for moving instead of dragging: a second direction of scrolling, a container of its own contrary to how `app` scrolls, and a separate text alternative;
- `skillchoose` right on a tap, and the details on hover and focus: an accidental tap would start a session, and a tablet has no hover;
- collapsing distant tiers, as the prototype does: in the first slice there is nothing to collapse, and a collapsed tier is one more state to keep and announce;
- one Tab stop and arrows across the tiles: a way of moving of the map's own, which has to be learned, for graphs of up to 17 skills;

## Ranks

**R07. In the first slice the map shows no ranks: what a rank is in NovaTrail is decided by О-13, and the conclusions of this detailing go there. When ranks come, they will be turned on by an attribute of the element, which `app` sets by the learner's presentation style, on both views of the map; without the attribute there are no ranks.** The author's answers of 2026-11-07 when planning T03 (RUN.md, T03, «Решено автором»), both by the plan's recommendations. How it is built: [architecture.md](architecture.md), section 3.5.
- **What the map shows of mastery** — the five states on the map, and for the adult also the mastery band by its title (R03). Neither a skill, nor a module, nor a learner has a rank.
- **What goes to О-13** — a row «В реестр spec» in table 3 of RUN.md. A learner cannot have a rank at all: the level is per module, and the scales of modules do not add up (concepts 11 spec). Two candidates remain:
  - the rank of a module — the number of the difficulty band in which the module's level stands, "rank k of N", as a number without a name, as in MathTrail (R213 standalone). `GetSkillMap` has no module level, so a field of the contract is needed, and a rule of `learner-model` by which the level falls into a band;
  - the rank of a skill — the number of its mastery band on the module's scale, from what `GetSkillMap` already gives.

  The row's recommendation is the rank of a module, if О-13 decides that ranks are visible, and until then none at all.
- **Turning on.** Ranks are part of the presentation style (concepts 13 spec; privacy 3.3 spec), and the map does not know the presentation style and does not read the profile (R04). So an attribute of the element turns them on, and `app` sets it. The attribute's name and the look of a rank on the map will be named by the entry that comes after the decision of О-13, as an amendment to R04. How the presentation style holds ranks — as a field of the profile or as a rule by age — is decided by О-13, О-23 and `app`.

— *Why:*
- the level of a module exists only inside `learner-model` (architecture `learner-model`, `main` e8f03bf, read 2026-11-07), and the estimate of a skill, `estimate`, is the level plus the skill's correction, and no view shows it (R03; `progress.proto` `spec`): the rank of a module cannot be derived from what the map has, and a field for a rank that О-13 has not decided on yet would be premature;
- the mastery band is the highest of the bands where the skill is taught (concepts 11 spec): a skill taught only in the lowest band would stay "rank 1" forever. Such a rank would speak of the module, not of the learner, and the map does not know the limit: the bands of tasks are closed ([format text](../spec/format/README.md), section 14);
- until the decision of О-13, `app` keeps a neutral tutor without ranks and without a persona (architecture 3.10 `app`, `main` 375a09b, read 2026-11-07): a map with ranks would diverge from the rest of the screen;
- product 4 spec requires showing what is mastered, what opens and what it builds on — it does not require a rank, and that much the map already shows (R03);
- the presentation style in the contract so far holds only voicing ([contracts text](../spec/contracts/README.md), section 15), and there is no list of styles (О-23): the map cannot decide for `app` what the style consists of.

— *Rejected:*
- the rank of a module as a field of `GetSkillMap` right now: a field in `v1` cannot be removed later, and whether ranks are needed О-13 has not decided yet;
- the rank of a skill by the mastery band: see "Why";
- a common rank of the learner across all modules, as in MathTrail: the scales of modules do not add up (concepts 11 spec);
- ranks always on the adult's view, and by an attribute on the learner's view: the adult sees the mastery band anyway (R03), and two rules of turning on would diverge from the presentation style, of which a profile has one.

— *Consequence:* R03 and R04 do not change: no view shows the estimate, as before, and there is no attribute for ranks yet. The row «В реестр spec» to О-13 is in table 3 of RUN.md. — RUN.md, T03, table 3; [architecture.md](architecture.md), sections 1, 2.3 and 3.5.

## The element

**R09. The element `<novatrail-skill-map>` builds and updates its tree by its own code, by skill ID: the assignments of one event-loop task merge into one update, a node is moved only when its place has changed, and the focus stays on it or goes by the rule of R06. The words are the package's own table with placeholders, for now only `en`. The English titles of the anchor skills are embedded from the format file of a release of `spec`, which `release-verify` checked once, and from then on its sha256 holds it. The styles are one `CSSStyleSheet` in `adoptedStyleSheets`, and a page under a strict CSP checks them. A trial consumer checks the package's declarations.** The author's answers of 2026-11-07 in planning T13 (RUN.md, T13, «Решено автором»): one task, the words `en`, embedding by the digest; the rest are the plan's proposals, which the author approved. How it is built — [architecture.md](architecture.md), section 3.3.
- **The tree** — `src/element/tree.ts`. A band is a `<section>` with a heading of the level `heading-level` and a `<ul>` of tiles. A tile is an `<li>` with a `<button>`: `aria-expanded`, and the expanded one also has `aria-controls` pointing to the details. The details are an `<li id="details">` right after the tile (R06). The nodes of the bands and tiles live in a `Map` keyed by the band and the skill ID, and new data change their text and attributes in place. A node is moved only when its place in the list has changed. If the focus was on it, the focus returns to it. If the node that had the focus is gone, the focus goes to the tile that the core's `focusAfter` names (T12). The button "Practise" stays in the details as long as it is needed — it is not recreated, or the focus would leave it.
- **Assignments** — the setters `graph`, `tiers`, `manifest` and `skillMap` and the changes of the attributes `view`, `lang` and `heading-level` queue one update in a microtask: `app` assigns the data one after another, and the map follows them once. Without `graph`, `tiers` or `manifest` the map is empty. A property that the page assigned before the element was registered lies on it as an own property and hides the setter: the constructor removes it and assigns it again through the setter. Parsing `view` and `heading-level` is in the core, `src/core/attributes.ts`, by R04 with its amendment.
- **State and choice** — from the core's `mapStates` (T11) and `layout` and `linksOf` (T12). "Practise" is there only in the learner's view, with `skillMap`, on a module's skill that is open. It dispatches `skillchoose` from the host — `bubbles`, `composed` and a frozen `detail` `{ skill }`, the full ID for `ChooseNext` (R04). A locked skill has "Waits for" and its prerequisites not mastered, in the order of the layout, as "Builds on" has: the graph's order has no meaning ([the format text](../spec/format/README.md), section 4).
- **The tile's accessible name** is its text: the title, then the state or the mark, then the link's label, separated by commas. The tile's parts are inline: Chromium surrounds a block or an out-of-flow element with spaces, and the name would become "Quarters , Locked". How the tile looks is decided by T14 — already under this constraint.
- **Words** — `src/element/words.ts`: the table `en`, and phrases with something substituted are templates with named places, `{tier}`, `{skills}`, `{band}`. The tree fills them with text or with nodes with their own `lang`, so that a skill's title and a tier's name in a phrase are in the explanation language, and no phrase is glued from pieces (R91 spec). The map's container has the `lang` of the words' language, `en`, so that a screen reader does not read them by the language of `app`'s page (3.1.2 WCAG 2.2). `ru`, CLDR's plural forms, choosing the language by the attribute `lang`, and the icons — T14.

## Lines

**R11. The lines of the expanded skill are cubic curves in one SVG under the map's bands: from the middle of the bottom edge of a prerequisite's tile to the top edge of the expanded tile, and from the bottom edge of its details to the middle of the top edge of each tile that the skill opens. The ends on the expanded tile and under its details are spread across the tile's width. The element measures the lines' places after each render of the tree and again on `ResizeObserver`. There is no animation.** The author's answers of 2026-11-07 in planning T15 (RUN.md, T15, «Решено автором»): a curve, recomputing on render and by the observer, no animation. The rest are the plan's proposals, which the author approved.
- **Geometry** — the core's `linesOf`, `src/core/lines.ts`, without DOM: from the rectangles of the tiles and the details in the map's coordinates, by the links of `linksOf` (T12) in the order of the layout. The curve is `M sx sy C sx my, ex my, ex ey`, where `my` is the middle in height: the line leaves the start and enters the end vertically. The ends on the expanded tile are at the fractions `(i + 1) / (n + 1)` of its width, separately for the lines to it and from it, in the order from left to right in which the other ends stand: so the points do not stick together, and the lines do not cross needlessly — both when the bands wrap and on a right-to-left page. The ends and the curve are accurate to 0.1 CSS px, rounded in one place. A tile without a place gives no line.
- **Drawing** — `src/element/lines.ts`: one `<svg class="lines">`, first in the map's container, `aria-hidden`. Each line has a `<g>` with `data-from` and `data-to`, a `<path>` and two points `<circle r="4">`. The drawing's unit is the map's CSS px: the SVG has no `viewBox`, and the style sets its size. The places are only SVG attributes, `d`, `cx` and `cy`; there is no attribute `style`, and the check under the strict CSP expands a skill and sees two lines without violations (R09). The lines' nodes live by the pair of ends and are moved in place.
- **Under the tiles.** The SVG is absolute over the whole container, `.map` is `position: relative`, and the bands are positioned too and come after it in the DOM: the tiles and the details, with the paper background, lie over the lines, and the lines are visible in the gaps between them. `pointer-events: none` — a touch goes through to the tile.
- **Recomputing.** At its end, `MapTree.render` measures the tiles and the details through `getBoundingClientRect` relative to the map and draws the lines. `ResizeObserver` watches the map, the details and each tile — it takes a tile when the tile is created and lets it go when it is gone — and draws the lines again if a skill is expanded: the page's width, the zoom and a font that finished loading change the tiles' size, and a tile shifted by a neighbour is shifted because the neighbour changed size. The observer does not watch the SVG itself, and drawing the lines does not change the sizes of what is observed — there is no loop. The element got `connectedCallback` and `disconnectedCallback`: removed from the page, it observes nothing, and once back, it observes again, and the observer's first notice draws the lines again.
- **Color** — the theme's accent, `--novatrail-skill-map-accent` (R10): `color` on the SVG, and the line of 2 px and the points are `currentColor`. So an invalid value of the accent gives the lines the ink's color, as it does the tiles, and not an empty `stroke`. The default has 6.7:1 to the paper, and a test holds no less than 3:1 to the map's background (1.4.11). In the forced colors mode — Highlight, as the borders of the linked tiles.
- **No animation** — the lines appear at once, and `prefers-reduced-motion` has nothing to turn off (2.3.3). The item of R10 "the motion to come — the lines of T15 — goes only under `(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`" is answered by this: no motion came.

— *Why:*
- a curve — as on MathTrail's topic map ([architecture.md](architecture.md), section 4): smooth lines merge less often, and one skill's lines are few;
- the geometry in the core — tests without a browser check it, and the element only measures and draws, as with the layout (R06);
- the observer on the tiles, not only on the map — at the same width of the map, a tile changes size with the font and the language;
- no animation — the link is visible at once, and 2.3.3 asks for neither a test project of its own nor waiting.

— *Rejected:*
- a polyline with right angles — its horizontal segments lie on top of one another in one gap between bands;
- the event of `document.fonts` as well — the observer sees a font finishing loading by the tiles' size;
- a fade-in over 150 ms under `no-preference` — one more test project for motion that the map does not ask for;
- all the ends at the middle of the edge — the points of several lines would merge into one;
- the ends in the order of the layout — when the bands wrap, the lines would cross needlessly.

— *Consequence:* T16 tries the lines by hand on a tablet and on a graph with many tiers; Д3 — collapsing distant tiers — stays.
